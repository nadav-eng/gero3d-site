About Gero3D

Short: Gero3D is an Israeli orthopedic technology company that turns an iPad or iPhone scan of a patient's limb into an automatically designed, 3D-printed cast.

Long: Gero3D (Gero) builds software for personalized orthopedics. Clinics scan the injured limb with the Gero app on an iPad or iPhone; the Gero design engine generates a cast fitted to that one patient in about 40 seconds, a task that takes about 45 minutes by hand; and certified production partners print it and ship it to the clinic within 3 days. The cast weighs about 60 g, compared with 1.5 to 2 kg for traditional plaster, is water-resistant and comes off without a saw. Gero3D was founded in 2023 and is based in Tel Aviv.

Key facts

  • Founded July 2023, Tel Aviv, Israel. Founder and CEO: Nadav Ichay.
  • More than 500 patient treatments on the Gero platform.
  • The Gero app runs on iPad and, in its new version, also on iPhone.
  • Automatic cast design in about 40 seconds, instead of about 45 minutes by hand.
  • Cast weight about 60 g, vs. 1.5 to 2 kg for a plaster cast. Water-resistant.
  • Quality management system certified to ISO 13485:2016 by the Standards Institution of Israel (certificate 1123048).
  • The ONE Splint, a ready-made 3D-printed wrist splint, is registered with the Israel Ministry of Health Medical Devices Division (AMAR) and listed with the U.S. FDA.
  • Agreement with Maccabi Healthcare Services in Israel, and pilots with clinics in the United States.
  • In development: a 3D-printed orthotics and prosthetics (O&P) service, including ankle-foot orthoses.

Founder

Nadav Ichay, Co-Founder & CEO. More than 10 years in 3D printing and the founder of three companies in the field, focused on bringing technology and medical wearables together.

Advisory board: Prof. Doron Norman, director of the orthopedic unit at Rambam Health Care Campus.
