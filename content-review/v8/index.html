The Gero platform

Software is the product. The cast is its output.

Four layers built in-house turn a clinic visit into a manufactured, patient-specific medical device, with no plaster room and no specialist skills.

  1. 01Scan

    Gero App

    An iPad app with a structured-light sensor captures the limb in under two minutes, accurate to 1 mm. The clinician enters patient details, picks the cast type and orders in the same visit.

    iOS · SwiftUIStructure SDKOffline-safe capture
  2. 02Design

    Design Engine

    Computer vision finds 21 anatomical keypoints, builds a hand skeleton and extracts the limb's contours. A parametric model then fits the cast to the patient and applies the chosen pattern.

    Computer visionParametric geometry45 min → 40 s design
  3. 03Manage

    Cloud platform

    A serverless backend runs orders, clinics, payments and patient notifications. An admin console gives the team full control of the catalogue, orders and support.

    AWS serverlessAdmin consoleSMS payment links
  4. 04Print

    Production network

    Print-ready files go to certified additive-manufacturing partners. Casts are printed, finished and shipped to the clinic within 3 days.

    ISO 13485 processesIndustrial 3D printing3-day delivery

From scan to model

One visit, one tablet, one order

The clinician scans, reviews the 3D model on screen and taps Done. The design engine takes it from there: the cast is generated, checked and sent to print automatically.

Made by additive manufacturing

From powder bed to patient

Every Gero cast is printed layer by layer from a file our software generated for one patient. Drag the handle to see a cast straight out of the printer, and the finished product.

Industrial powder-bed printing makes the open rhombus lattice possible: strong where the fracture needs support, open everywhere else for air and water.

In the clinic

Four steps, one app

The clinic receives an iPad with the Gero app installed. After a short training session, staff scan patients and order casts on their own.

  1. 01

    scan

    The patient's limb is scanned with the Gero app. A ONE Splint goes on for the first days.

  2. 02

    customize

    Choose the cast type, pattern and colour, and preview the result in the app.

  3. 03

    print

    The design engine generates the cast and it's 3D printed by our production network.

  4. 04

    heal

    The cast arrives within 3 days and replaces the splint in seconds. Removal needs no saw.

The Gero advantage

Better for patients, simpler for clinics

Lightweight

About 60 g, against almost 2 kg for a traditional plaster cast.

Water resistant

Patients can shower and swim with it, with their doctor's approval.

Hygienic

Open, ventilated structure that keeps skin healthy and is easy to clean.

Custom fit

Built from a 3D scan of the patient's own limb, for comfort through healing.

Enhanced mobility

Thin and light, so patients stay active in daily life.

Less mess

No plaster, no water basins. The casting area stays clean.

Saves time

Scan, customize and order from one app. A two-minute workflow.

Personal style

Patients choose the pattern and colour of their own cast.
